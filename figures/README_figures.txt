FIGURE FILES
------------
The TIFF files in this folder are the final versions supplied to PLOS ONE.

    Fig1.tif  WTA/WTP survey-box illustration; not a map.
    Fig2.tif  Blank-background drive-time map; underlying location-revealing
              coordinates and contours are withheld.
    Fig3.tif  Blank-background drive-time map; underlying location-revealing
              coordinates and contours are withheld.
    Fig4.tif  Speed versus distance with fitted Spillman curve.
    Fig5.tif  Speed versus distance with fitted Spillman curve.

Figures 1-3 are supplied final images and are not analysis outputs. Figures 4-5
can be regenerated from data/speed_distance_deidentified.csv by running
code/figs4_5_spillman_R_only.R from data/. The regenerated copies are written to
data/ and do not replace the final copies in this folder.
